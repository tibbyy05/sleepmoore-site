The page that falls asleep.

Ten motion ideas for sleepmoore.app, all live. Scroll slowly: the sky behind this page runs from 9 pm to sunrise, a sheep counts every idea you pass, and the cord at the top right turns the lights out.

01

The scroll is one night

Look behind these cards. The sky darkens, the stars come out and the moon crosses as you scroll. The clock in the top corner is the scroll bar. The bottom of the page is sunrise.

Why it stands out: the whole site becomes one story, from bedtime to waking, instead of a pile of sections.

02

The page breathes

A flower of soft light opens for 4 seconds while you breathe in, then folds closed for 6 while you breathe out. Specks of light drift in with each breath and float away as you let it go. That's 6 breaths a minute, a slow, calming pace.

Why it stands out: visitors calm down without being told to. The motion does the work.

03

Sound you can see

Press play. The picture listens to the real sound: rain runs down the glass, the stove flickers with each crackle, fireflies pulse with the crickets and wind pushes mist across the valley. Turn a slider down and that part of the picture fades.

Why it stands out: it's proof the app is real, and nobody can copy it with stock video. Wind even tilts the rain.

Sound plays only while this section is on screen.
04

Your real sky, right now

This sky uses your own clock. The colours match your time of day, and the moon is tonight's real phase in roughly the right place. Drag the slider to see other hours.

Why it stands out: the site looks different at 11 pm than at lunch, so it always feels made for that moment.

05

Words that fall asleep

Leave the heading alone and it slowly nods off: letters droop, lean on each other, and little z's drift up. Hover or tap and it wakes with a stretch.

Why it stands out: it has character and makes people smile. It could live on the hero heading or the 404 page.

06

Counting sheep

Look at the bottom of your screen. Press and hold the sheep and carry it over the fence yourself, with a mouse or a finger. Too low and it bumps into the fence; let go and it drops back to the grass. In Manual (the default) you can also tap the little field at the bottom of the screen. In Automatic, sheep jump the fence one after another until you press Pause.

Why it stands out: a tiny joke everyone gets at once, and it gives the page a reading-progress bar.

07

Build your scene

Drag a sound into the night. A campfire lights where you drop it, rain clouds roll in, a stream starts to run, and you hear each one. Tap anything you placed to remove it.

Why it stands out: the mixer becomes a little world you build yourself. It's play, not settings.

08

Dream-like changes

Nothing cuts or slides. Shapes melt into the next thing, the way scenes change in a dream: the cabin window becomes the moon, and the moon becomes the play button. Tap to step through it.

Why it stands out: moving between pages feels like drifting, not clicking. On the site, a tapped world card would melt into the player.

10

Pull the cord: light mode to dark mode

The page starts lamp-lit, with cream paper cards over the night. Pull the cord at the top right (drag it down or tap it) and the lamp clicks off: everything fades to night and the fireflies come out. Pull again to switch the lamp back on.

Why it stands out: a dark-mode switch everyone understands without a label, and people pull it again and again just to watch. On the real site it could also start a sleep sound.
