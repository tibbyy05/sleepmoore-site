Sleep Moore

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Sleep Moore is a sleep sounds app. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to information when you use it.

The short version

  • Everything you set up in the app stays on your phone.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, health data or anything you type.
  • The only thing that leaves your phone is crash and performance diagnostics, so we can fix problems. These reports do not include your name or anything that identifies you.
  • We do not sell or share data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What stays on your phone

The app saves these on your device only. We never receive them:

  • Your saved worlds and the names you give them, and the sounds and levels in each.
  • Your settings: sleep timer length, fade-out length, night arc and bed mode on or off, the last world you looked at, your Night Journey and breathing choices.
  • The time you need to be up, how much sleep you want, and whether the bedtime reminder is on.
  • Your recent nights (up to about two months): which world played, when, for how long, and how you said you slept the next morning.
  • Your answer to the first-launch question.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Crash and performance diagnostics

The app uses EAS Observe, a service from Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), to report crashes, errors and how quickly the app starts and responds. A report can include:

  • The error and where in the app it happened.
  • How long the app took to open and to respond, and whether the screen stuttered.
  • Your phone model, operating system and version, language setting, app version, battery level and whether low power mode is on, and the type of network connection (for example Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  • A random identifier created for this install of the app, so reports from the same install can be grouped. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, Google account or advertising ID, and it is reset if you delete and reinstall the app.

Reports do not contain your saved worlds, the names you typed, your sleep notes or anything else you entered. We use them only to find and fix problems. Expo processes them on our behalf; see Expo's privacy policy. Diagnostics are sent only from installed builds of the app, not from the web version.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): asked for only when you turn on the bedtime reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; no message comes from a server and we do not receive a notification token.
  • Background audio: lets the sounds keep playing with the screen locked and show playback controls on the lock screen. The name and picture of the world playing are shown there by your phone.
  • Microphone: Sleep Moore does not ask for or use the microphone.

Sharing a mix

If you choose Share, the app creates a link that contains the sounds, their levels and the name of the mix, and hands it to your phone's share sheet. It goes only where you send it. We do not receive it.

Children

Sleep Moore is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing to delete; if you have a question, contact us below.

Your choices and rights

All data the app stores is on your device, and you can remove it at any time by deleting the app. Diagnostic reports cannot be tied back to you, so we cannot look them up by person. If you have a question or a request under data protection law (such as GDPR or CCPA), write to us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle information differently (for example a sound recorder or an optional account), we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in the app's release notes.
