Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for. Everything you set up stays on your phone.
Will the sounds keep playing with the screen locked?
Yes. Sounds play in the background, and you can pause them from the lock screen.
The gentle wake alarm did not sound.
Leave a world playing with "Play until", and keep notifications on for Sleep Moore so a backup chime can sound if the app was closed. Check that the ringer is not on silent.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Everything it stored is on your phone and goes with it. See the privacy policy for details.
