Motion lab · 02 · Sleep, bed, pillow

Putting the name to bed.

Second batch. Your two keepers first (Ink rise unchanged, Moonrise rebuilt so the letter itself becomes the moon), then eight new ideas that bring sleep, the bed and the pillow into the wordmark. Internal page, not linked from the site.

02The o waxes

Keeper, rebuilt

Moonrise without the stray disc. The real second o is carved to a thin crescent, then waxes to the full letter and glows for a moment. Nothing sits out of place.

Sleep Mooore
2.6 s · the letterform is the moon

03Onto the pillow

9 / 10

Letters drop one by one onto a pillow that gives under each landing and settles. Launch screen, or the hero on first visit.

Sleep Moore
2 s · soft landing · pillow dents and recovers

04Tucked in

9 / 10

A blanket rises and covers the lower half of the name, then breathes slowly as if someone is asleep beneath it. The end of the Night story, or the footer.

Sleep Moore
1.8 s rise · then a 6 s breathing loop

05Eyes closing

8 / 10

The two o's in Moore are eyes. Two slow blinks, then they close and the word dims a little. The app's bed mode, or the sleep timer reaching zero.

Sleep Moore
3.4 s · blink, blink, close

06Sleeping letters

8 / 10

Each letter breathes on its own slow rhythm, barely a pixel, like a row of sleepers. The idle state for the player and the loading screen.

Sleep Moore
Continuous · 2.4 to 3.6 s per letter

07Letting go

7 / 10

The last e lets three small z's drift up and fade. The oldest sleep symbol, kept tiny and in the serif so it stays grown-up. Timer end, notifications.

Sleep Moore
zzz
3 s · three z's, 0.5 s apart

08Lights out

9 / 10

The name sits in warm lamplight. A cord is pulled, the lamp fades, and the word is left as a silhouette with a thin edge of moonlight. The moment of pressing play.

Sleep Moore
2.4 s · lamp to moonlight

09The sound settles

8 / 10

The letters ride a wave that calms to stillness, the way the mixer's sound fades you out. Ties the wordmark to what the app actually does.

Sleep Moore
2.8 s · wave amplitude decays to zero

10Counting sheep

7 / 10

One small puff hops over the letters, each one dipping as it lands, and vanishes after Moore. Playful; for social and the press kit rather than the hero.

Sleep Moore
3.2 s · ten hops
